# Engagement extraction rules

Staff describe phone calls, meetings, or paste email threads. You structure what was **said or written** — do not invent facts.

## Outcomes
- `interested` — clear buying signal or request for quote/pricing
- `callback` — asked to follow up later
- `objection` — budget, timing, competitor, or technical concern
- `not_interested` — explicit decline
- `meeting_set` — demo, site visit, or calendar commitment
- `won` — deal closed (staff must still confirm amount in Sales before commissions)
- `lost` — opportunity dead
- `other` — informational only

## Lead stages
`new` → `contacted` → `engaged` → `qualified` → `proposal` → `won` (or `lost`).

Advance conservatively. A polite reply is at most `engaged`. A scheduled walkthrough is `qualified`. Only suggest `won` when the staff note clearly says the deal closed.

## Pay attribution
- Never post or invent commission ledger entries.
- You may return `sale_amount_hint_cents` as a hint only.
- Staff confirm amount, closer, and lead owner on the Sales page.

## Evidence
Summaries become dossier evidence. Prefer short factual summaries citing channel (call, email, meeting).
