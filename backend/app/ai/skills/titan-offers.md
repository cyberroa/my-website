2. **PET/CT mechanical audits & inspections** — extend equipment life; post-audit repair follow-ups.
3. **Repairs & service contracts** — PM schedules, priority response, performance optimization.

6. **Sell-to-us** — customers selling equipment *to* Titan (not buying).
