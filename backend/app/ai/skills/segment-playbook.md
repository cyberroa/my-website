# Writing a segment playbook

A segment playbook persists context for a subset of customers so campaigns stay effective.

Include:
- Who they are (ICP narrative)
- Why they matter to Titan now
- Recommended Titan offers (audits, parts, used/new systems, contracts)
- Messaging angles and proof points
- Competitor counterpoints / price sensitivity
- Do-not-say / compliance notes

Keep it actionable for AI Studio and email/outreach drafts. Update labels when the subset meaning changes.
