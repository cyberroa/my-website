# Evidence rules

Nothing about a person is guessed. Tools report what was **observed**.

- **Strong evidence** may write structured fields (tags, notes, role, company, phone, website, source) when the observation comes from the customer's own timeline, forms, or service jobs.
- **Weak evidence** becomes a suggestion a human settles. Never invent confidence scores.
- Prefer citing the source event, form, or listing in the observation text.
- A confidently wrong fact is worse than a blank field.
